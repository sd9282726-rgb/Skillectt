1. Overview

Skillect (“we”, “our”, or “the app”) is a growth-focused social platform. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: email address and password (handled by our authentication provider; passwords are not stored in plain text).

  • Content: posts, comments, images, videos, and other material you upload or share.
  • Interactions: likes, follows, messages, community memberships, and similar activity.
  • Technical data: basic logs needed to operate and secure the service (for example device or connection information).

3. How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you
  • To provide social features (profiles, feed, posts, comments, follows, communities, messages)

  • To respond to support requests

4. Storage and service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage. Data is processed in the eu-west-2 region (Europe) unless otherwise required for operation.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Content you choose to post may be visible to other Skillect users. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and integrity of the service.

6. Age requirement

Skillect is intended for users 13 years of age and older. Users must confirm eligibility when creating an account.

7. Your choices

  • You may request account deletion by emailing sd9282726@gmail.com.
