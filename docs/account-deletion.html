Account Deletion — Skillect

You can request permanent deletion of your Skillect account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

Email: sd9282726@gmail.com

Use the subject line: Skillect Account Deletion Request

Include:

  • The email address registered on your Skillect account
  • Your username (if you remember it)
  • A clear statement that you want the account deleted

What gets deleted

  • Your account login and profile information
  • Your posts, comments, and uploaded media where technically feasible
  • Follows, likes, and similar account-linked activity data

Processing time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some backup copies may take longer to fully clear.

Verification

We may ask you to confirm the request from your registered email so we do not delete the wrong account.
