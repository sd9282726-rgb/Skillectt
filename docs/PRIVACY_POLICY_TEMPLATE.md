# Privacy Policy — Skillect

**Last updated:** November 1, 2026  
**Contact:** [YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL]

## Overview

Skillect ("we", "our", "the app") is a growth-focused social platform. This policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.

## Data we collect

- **Account:** email address, password (hashed by our auth provider)
- **Profile:** display name, username, bio, location, skills, avatar
- **Content:** posts, comments, images/videos you upload
- **Interactions:** likes, follows, messages, community memberships
- **Technical:** basic device/app logs needed to operate the service

## How we use data

- Provide authentication and the social features of the app
- Show your profile and content to other users according to the product design
- Improve reliability and security
- Respond to support requests

## Storage and processors

Data is stored using **Supabase** (database, auth, file storage), region **eu-west-2** (Europe).

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Content you post is visible to other users of Skillect. We may disclose information if required by law.

## Age

Skillect is intended for users **13 years and older**. Users must confirm age eligibility at signup.

## Your choices

- Edit or delete your own posts and profile fields in the app
- Request account deletion by emailing [YOUR_SUPPORT_EMAIL]
- Report or block other users using in-app tools where available

## Security

We use industry-standard practices including encrypted transport (HTTPS) and provider-managed authentication. No method of transmission is 100% secure.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
